# Hardware Components

## Arduino Uno
Acts as the main controller and processes data received from all sensors.

## DHT22 Temperature and Humidity Sensor
Measures room temperature and relative humidity.

## MQ Gas Sensor
Detects changes in gas concentration and identifies potential gas leakage.

## HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor
Measures distance and detects objects entering the defined proximity range.

## Green LED
Indicates normal and safe operating conditions.

## Yellow LED
Indicates a high-temperature warning.

## Red LED
Indicates a critical safety condition such as gas leakage or object detection.

## Piezo Buzzer
Provides an audible warning when a safety threshold is exceeded.
